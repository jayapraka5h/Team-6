02

About LLP

The entity in one view

✓ LLP means Limited Liability Partnership.

✓ It is a separate legal entity under LLP Act, 2008.

✓ Partners generally have limited liability.

✓ Minimum 2 partners; maximum can be unlimited.

✓ Can do business, earn profit and continue for a long time.

✓ Account opened in the name of LLP, not partners.

Simple Meaning

The LLP is the business entity. Partners run the business, but their liability is limited.

ADVANTAGES & LIMITATIONS

Advantages & Limitations of LLP

Advantages

✓

Limited Liability

Partners generally have limited liability for LLP obligations.

✓

Separate Legal Entity

LLP has a legal identity separate from its partners.

✓

Flexible Management

Partners can manage the business according to the LLP Agreement.

✓

Flexible Profit Sharing

Partners can decide the profit-sharing ratio through the LLP Agreement.

Limitations

!

Compliance Requirements

An LLP has registration and ongoing compliance requirements.

!

Minimum Two Partners

An LLP requires at least two partners.

!

Partner-Based Structure

Its functioning depends on the roles and responsibilities of its partners.

!

Agreement is Important

Rights, duties and profit sharing depend heavily on the LLP Agreement.
